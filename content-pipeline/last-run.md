## 1. kb-0139 — sale-purchase-agreement-earnest-money-ccc-thailand

**Title:** Earnest money and backing out: what Thai contract law says about
your villa's sale agreement
**faqCategory:** documents

**Sources used:**
- Thailand Law Online — CCC Sections 453-457 and 386-394, fetched directly
  (Section 456's two-tier rule: a registered final sale vs. an enforceable-by-
  three-alternative-routes agreement to sell; Sections 386-388 rescission
  notice-and-cure vs. time-is-of-the-essence mechanics).
- Thailand Law Library (Siam Legal) — CCC Sections 377-385 (earnest's dual
  evidentiary/security function under Section 377; Section 378's default
  forfeit-vs-return rule).
- ASEAN Secretariat's own hosted full English translation of CCC Book III
  (asean.org) — primary/official cross-check of the same sections.

Framed conservatively: explicitly noted Section 378 does not entitle a buyer
to double the earnest back absent an explicit contract term, since some
practitioner content blurs a common negotiated add-on with the statutory
default.
