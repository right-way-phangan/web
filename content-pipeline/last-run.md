## Guide 2

- **slug:** `specific-business-tax-five-year-exemption-clock`
- **kbId:** `kb-0084`
- **title (EN):** Specific Business Tax's 5-year exemption: how the clock is counted and which sales qualify
- **title (RU):** Пятилетнее освобождение от специального налога на бизнес: как считается срок и какие продажи под него подпадают
- **faqCategory:** `costs`
- **faqQuestion:** How is the 5-year Specific Business Tax exemption counted when I sell my Koh Phangan property?
- **Sources used:**
  - The Revenue Department of Thailand (rd.go.th) — official SBT page; confirmed the "commercial or profitable" statutory basis (Section 91/2(6)) and cross-checked the combined rate against secondary sources below (the RD's own rate-schedule table returned an inconsistent 0.1% figure for real estate that conflicts with the well-corroborated 3%+10% figure already used in the site's published `withholding-tax-property-sale-individual-vs-company` guide, so the higher-confidence 3.3% figure — matching Revenue Code Section 91/6(6) and every secondary legal source checked — was used)
  - Royal Decree No. 342 (B.E. 2541), issued under the Revenue Code — the decree that actually defines which property sales count as "commercial or profitable" for SBT; full text is a scanned/non-extractable PDF, so its existence, number and effective date (1 Jan 1999) were confirmed via search but exemption specifics were sourced from the secondary legal analyses below
  - MSNA Group — Specific Business Tax Criteria on Real Estate in Thailand — 3.3% combined rate, the 5-year holding rule (counted from the most recent of land/building acquisition dates), the house-registration (tabien baan) one-year exemption including for foreign owners on the yellow book, and the inheritance/expropriation/child-gift exemption list
  - Terms.Law — Thailand property transfer taxes — corroborated that the 5-year clock starts at title registration (not the purchase contract date) and that SBT and stamp duty are mutually exclusive on the same transfer
